HoverLingua / Privacy

Your text stays
on your Mac.

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This policy describes the current HoverLingua app and its pages on apps.rinda.co. HoverLingua is published by Rinda. Privacy questions can be sent to developer@rinda.co.

Screen captures and text

When you choose Capture & Translate, HoverLingua captures your selected screen region using Apple's ScreenCaptureKit. It uses Vision to recognize text and Apple Translation to translate supported language pairs. This processing happens on your Mac. HoverLingua does not send screenshots, recognized text, or translations to Rinda or a third-party translation service.

The app holds the current capture and result as needed to display them. It does not save captures or translation history to disk. Text you choose to copy or share outside the app is handled by the destination you choose.

Permissions and language models

Screen Recording access is used to capture text displayed by other apps. HoverLingua's capture configuration disables audio, and the app does not request camera, microphone, or Accessibility access. You can manage or revoke Screen Recording permission in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Apple manages language-model downloads. An initial download requires a connection to Apple's services. Once supported models are installed, translation runs on device. Apple's services and any system diagnostics you choose to share are governed by Apple's privacy information.

Accounts, analytics, and diagnostics

HoverLingua currently has no accounts, advertising, custom analytics, or third-party crash-reporting SDK. Mixpanel and Sentry are not included in the current app. If a future release adds analytics or diagnostic reporting, this policy and the app's disclosures will be updated to describe that release's behavior and choices.

These website pages

This website uses no analytics scripts, advertising cookies, embedded trackers, or contact forms. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes technical information associated with serving the site, such as visitors' IP addresses and request information, under its privacy statement. Following an external link takes you to a service with its own privacy practices.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and anything you attach. We use that information to answer your request and troubleshoot the issue. Please avoid sending private screenshots or captured text unless needed, and redact anything you don't want to share.

Support messages are kept while needed to handle the request and any follow-up, or where retention is required by law. Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information you have provided. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights, including the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when the app or our handling of information changes. The date above identifies the latest version.
